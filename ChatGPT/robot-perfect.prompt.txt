It should read as cold, clinical and unfeeling next to them, in the same style family.

Scene/backdrop: genuine transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no floor, no shadow.
Style/medium: crisp flat 2D icon, flat colour areas, clean smooth contours, readable at 64 pixels. No painted lighting, gloss, gradients or glow halos. Same shape language and line weight as robot-blue.png and robot-red.png.
Subject details: angular steel-grey #8a8983 head with slightly darker #5f5e5a panel lines, visor slit in ice blue #7fc4ee, off-white #f6f5f1 only for tiny highlights. Flat colour only.
Color palette: steel grey dominates; ice blue for the visor only. No warm colours anywhere.
Composition/framing: one centered head on a square 1024 x 1024 canvas, fully visible with about 10 percent transparent padding on all sides.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no body, no arms, no background shapes, no enclosing circle or badge, no watermark, no checkerboard. Output a transparent PNG.
Project destination: ChatGPT/robot-perfect.png in the AI Brain project.
