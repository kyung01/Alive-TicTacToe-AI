Live battle: the latest brain vs itself

Both players run the newest saved brain (same weights, separate thoughts) and tick through the whole game; a move is whatever output crosses the threshold, whenever that happens. An illegal fire (out of turn, or on a taken cell) does nothing: the board ignores it and the game keeps ticking. Sitting on your own turn for 30 ticks is a timeout, and the other side takes the game. X always plays on the left and moves first; the two bots swap marks (and sides) every game. The number above each head is the generation that brain was saved at.
