Every big AI lab says it's building AGI, a machine that thinks the way we do. This video argues that today's models can't get there. That isn't because they're too small or trained on the wrong data. It's because they're built in the wrong shape.

Problem one is structure. In a brain, signals loop forward, backward and sideways, and the activity never stops. Today's models are a one-way pipeline. Text goes in, passes through fixed layers once, and one word comes out the top.

Problem two is the thought itself. A thought isn't text, yet "let me think step by step" is just more text fed back into the same calculation.

So what would work? Stop trying to design a mind by hand and let it grow the way nature did: through evolution, with networks that loop and remember.
